About Cayuga Lake Wine Trail

Cayuga Lake Wine Trail is America's first wine trail. It has been in existence for more than 40 years, contributing to the preservation of wineries from past generations. With more than 130 wineries to choose from throughout the entire Finger Lakes Wine Region, wine enthusiasts can spend weeks exploring the different vineyards and sampling unique wines in the area. The Cayuga Lake Wine Trail helps you narrow down your travel list to a smaller group of established wineries to give you a personalized experience.
